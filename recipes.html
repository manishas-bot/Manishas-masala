Allergy-Safe Recipes

Sixteen dishes built with the same fusion spirit, checked for protein and fiber balance, and labeled clearly so your family can eat together without guesswork.

Mastering the Swap

  • Soaked raw sunflower or pumpkin seeds replace cashew and poppy seed pastes for creamy gravies.
  • Cornmeal (makki), finger millet (ragi), sorghum, or chickpea flour (besan) replace wheat flatbreads.
  • Store-bought hing (asafoetida) often hides wheat — look for certified gluten-free versions.
