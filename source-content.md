Here are **5 essential facts** every beginner needs when getting started with allergy-safe Indian cooking:

1. **Most Core Indian Dishes Are Naturally Gluten-Free**: Traditional mainstays like lentil *dals*, chickpea curries, vegetable dishes, and basmati rice recipes are naturally gluten-free because they are built around legumes, rice, and fresh vegetables.
2. **Watch for Hidden Wheat in Asafoetida (*Hing*)**: While pure spices are naturally gluten-free, commercial powdered asafoetida (*hing*) frequently uses wheat flour as a carrier, so celiac-safe cooks must look for certified gluten-free or compounded versions.
3. **Soaked Sunflower Seeds Make Rich, Nut-Free Creamy Bases**: You can achieve the velvety consistency of cashew-based *makhani* or *korma* gravies without tree nuts by blending soaked raw sunflower seeds or pumpkin seeds into a smooth paste.
4. **Wheat Flatbreads Have Easy Alternative Grain Swaps**: Traditional wheat-based rotis and naans can be replaced with naturally gluten-free flatbreads made from yellow cornmeal (*Makki ki Roti*), finger millet (*ragi*), sorghum, or chickpea flour (*besan*).
5. **Mild 4-Spice Blends Create Kid-Friendly Foundations**: Combining equal parts cumin, coriander, fennel, and mustard seeds yields an authentic, top-allergen-free spice mix that adds warm flavor without overwhelming young or spice-sensitive palates.
